# Delete your ApexHunt AI data or account

**Developer:** Sk Arif Majid · **App:** ApexHunt AI (com.apexhunt.ai)

## In the app (instant)
1. Open **ApexHunt AI**.
2. Go to the **AI & Settings** tab.
3. Tap **Delete account & wipe data** and confirm.

This erases everything stored on your phone (CV, profile, saved jobs, tailored documents, prep decks and your stored API key). If you signed in for Apex AI credits, it also deletes your account, credit balance, usage ledger and reports from our servers.

## Without the app
Email **sk.wedmist@gmail.com** from the address you signed in with, subject **"Delete my ApexHunt AI account"**. We'll confirm within 7 days and complete deletion within 30 days.

## What is deleted and what is kept
- **Deleted:** account record, email address, credit balance, subscription status record, AI usage ledger, AI-output reports.
- **Kept:** purchase records we must retain for tax or legal reasons (up to the legally required period). Google Play keeps its own purchase history.
- Unused AI credits are forfeited. Cancel any active subscription in **Google Play → Payments & subscriptions** first, otherwise Google will keep billing it.
