## Full description (≤4000)

```
Stop rewriting your CV for every application. ApexHunt AI finds jobs and freelance gigs around the world, tailors your real experience to each one, and gets you ready for the interview.

◆ DISCOVER — your AI job agent
• Search remote and on-site roles across 30+ countries: USA, UK, UAE, Germany, India, Canada, Australia and more
• Switch between salaried jobs and freelance projects (hourly or fixed-price)
• Every listing is ranked against your skills so the best matches come first
• Watch the career boards of companies you love
• Or paste any job description for an instant tailor

◆ TAILOR — honest, ATS-ready CVs in seconds
• Upload your CV once (PDF, DOCX or text)
• For each job, ApexHunt reads the requirements and rewrites your real experience in the employer's language
• Skill check-in: tell us what you've really used — it gets added in your own words; everything else stays off your CV
• Truth Guard double-checks every line so nothing you didn't do sneaks in
• See your ATS keyword match before and after, line by line
• Export a clean, single-column PDF and editable Word file that applicant tracking systems can read
• Get a matching cover letter — or a client-ready freelance proposal with milestones

◆ PREP — walk in confident
• A 60-second "tell me about yourself" pitch for every role
• STAR stories built from your own work
• The questions they're likely to ask, with answer outlines
• For every skill gap: a plain-English explainer, an honest way to answer "have you used X?", and a 48-hour learning plan with a practice project
• Flip cards, listen-mode audio and mastery tracking — all available offline

◆ YOUR DATA STAYS YOURS
• Your CV and applications are stored on your phone
• No ads. We never sell your data or train AI on your CV
• Delete your account and wipe your data in one tap

◆ PRICING
• Free to start, with welcome AI credits
• Top up Apex AI credits as you go — a tailored application usually costs a few cents
• ApexHunt Pro: use your own AI key (OpenAI, Anthropic, Google Gemini, Groq, OpenRouter or a local model), unlock the Apex Elite engine and get monthly bonus credits

ApexHunt AI is an AI assistant. Always review your documents before you send them. You are responsible for making sure every statement is accurate.

Job listings come from Remotive, Jobicy, Remote OK, Arbeitnow, Adzuna, Jooble, Greenhouse, Lever and Freelancer.com, and link back to the original posting.
```
